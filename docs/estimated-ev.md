# Estimated EV replaces public GI

All eight sport integrations use the shared estimated-ev helper. Public rankings,
featured picks, game slates and full player cards show Estimated EV instead of GI.
Legacy GI records remain available in historical administrative review; their
numbers are never relabelled or converted to EV.

EV% = 100 × (P(selected side wins) × net American-odds payout − P(side loses)).
Pushes return stake and contribute zero profit. Probabilities must be fractional,
finite, complete and sum to one. Match the original player, game, market, quote,
sportsbook, line, selection side and selection payout through savedModelPick.
The pregame quote must have been fresh when recorded. Retained EV describes
saved odds, not necessarily an offer still available today. Settled results never
supply the probability or side. Failed probability-validation markets remain
withheld; bookmaker-implied scorer chances cannot establish independent EV.

The shared forecast boundary saves an EV snapshot alongside newly generated
forecasts, so future frozen boards, archive snapshots and featured selections
retain its original probability, odds, quote ID, model-decision time and value.
Older rows may display EV derived solely from their intact original saved
forecast and quote; their historical source files are not rewritten. Missing
source evidence produces a dash plus a specific explanation, never invented EV.

Full player pages resolve saved evidence on the server. Client URL probability,
EV or odds values are not trusted. Multiple games or quotes require an exact
identity, unless the original locked featured record identifies the selection.

This is model-estimated EV, not proven profitability or a win probability. The
change does not repair probability accuracy, add scorer models, create quarter
forecasts from full-game data or approve experimental models as verified bets.
Hidden Soccer/WNBA/MLB dashboards remain hidden and collection continues.
